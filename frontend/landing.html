A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Some practical details before you begin.

Is GATEPath an official GATE or IIT website?＋

No. GATEPath is an independent preparation project and is not affiliated with IIT Madras, the GATE organising institute or any participating institution. Always verify official exam information on the official GATE website.

Does GATEPath support all GATE papers?＋

The paper catalogue is designed for all 30 GATE 2027 test papers. The platform is being developed in stages, so the availability and verification status of learning content may differ by paper until content rollout is complete.

Are all notes, questions and mock tests available now?＋

Not yet. The preparation workspace is being built module by module. Content that is not ready or verified will be clearly labelled instead of being presented as complete.

Can I change my selected paper later?＋

Yes. You can change your selected paper from your workspace preferences. Your learning space is designed around the paper you choose.

Will GATEPath predict my GATE rank or guarantee admission?＋

No. Any future score or rank estimates will be indicative only. No preparation platform can guarantee a GATE score, rank, admission or recruitment outcome.
