## Scoring reference

The GATE 2026 official master-paper/key page explains the answer-key conventions and scoring: incorrect MCQs incur one-third or two-thirds negative marks depending on mark value; MSQs have no negative marking or partial marking; NATs have no negative marking and are checked against the accepted key range.

Official source: https://gate2026.iitg.ac.in/QPs-answer-keys.html

## Content integrity and rights

- Original question wording, options, diagrams and figures are not copied into GATEPath. The learner opens the corresponding IIT-hosted PDF.
- GATEPath stores question identifiers, question type, section, marks, answer-key metadata, source URLs and editorial topic mappings.
- Worked explanations included in `CS-2026.json` are original GATEPath editorial notes. They are not copied from a coaching provider.
- Only reviewed topic mappings are populated. Other imported questions retain a null topic mapping until reviewed.
- No expected answer is returned by the list/detail API before an attempt is submitted.
- A question without an original reviewed explanation is shown as “editorial explanation pending”; it is not presented as a fully solved question.
