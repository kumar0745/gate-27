# Stage 9 — Content sources and provenance

## Official GATE 2027 pattern

- Source: IIT Madras, GATE 2027 Question Paper Pattern.
- URL: https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/question_paper_pattern
- Verified: 6 November 2026.
- The page specifies a computer-based examination, English language, 3-hour duration, 30 test papers, 10 General Aptitude + 55 subject questions (65 total), MCQ/MSQ/NAT types, 1- or 2-mark questions, and the paper-group-specific distribution of marks.
- It specifies one-third negative marking for a wrong 1-mark MCQ and two-thirds for a wrong 2-mark MCQ; no negative marking for MSQ/NAT; no partial marking in MSQ.
- It lists the special common/optional section choices for AR, GE, GG, RA, XE, XH and XL.

## CS mock paper content

- File: `backend/app/data/mocks/CS-FULL-MOCK-01.json`.
- Origin: GATEPath-authored original mock content created for the product pilot.
- It is not an official GATE 2027 paper, not transcribed from a previous-year question paper and not intended to predict examination questions.
- Its section-level marks follow the published CS distribution: GA 15, Engineering Mathematics 13 and remaining CS subject questions 72. The selected number of questions assigned within the 13-mark and 72-mark portions is an authored blueprint choice.
- All question statements, options, answer keys and worked explanations require independent subject-matter-expert review before the mock is described as expert-verified or used in a high-stakes paid test product.
- During a final consistency audit, the FCFS average waiting-time question's numeric key was aligned to its worked solution: 13/3 ms, within the accepted range.

## Important distinction

The official source validates the examination pattern and marking rules. It does not endorse GATEPath's original mock questions, answer keys or explanations. GATEPath is an independent preparation project and is not affiliated with IIT Madras or the GATE organising institute.
