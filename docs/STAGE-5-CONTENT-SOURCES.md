# Stage 5 — Content provenance and external learning resources

Content source review date: 2026-11-06.

## Primary syllabus source

- GATE 2027 official test papers and revised syllabus catalogue (IIT Madras): https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/exam_papers_and_syllabus
- Official GATE 2027 CS syllabus PDF: https://gate2027.iitm.ac.in/static/doc/GATE2027_Syllabus/CS_GATE2027_Syllabus.pdf

The topic IDs and scope statements in the CS notes map to the GATEPath CS syllabus JSON, which records the official PDF URL and transcription status. The explanations, examples, formula explanations and revision reminders are original GATEPath editorial content; they are not copied from the official PDF or represented as official guidance.

## Copyright and licensing policy

- Link to third-party courses and provider pages; do not mirror or download course videos unless the provider license explicitly permits it.
- Recommend textbooks by title and author; do not distribute scans or copyrighted PDFs without permission.
- Only label a question as an official GATE PYQ after verifying its year, paper, question number and source against an official paper/answer-key source. Stage 5 examples are not PYQs.
